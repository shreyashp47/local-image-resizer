Local Image Resizer

🔒 Privacy policy

Your images never leave your device

Local Image Resizer is a fully client-side tool. There is no backend, no upload endpoint, and no analytics that transmit image data.

What happens to your image

  • Files are read locally via FileReader / createObjectURL.
  • Processing uses the browser's Canvas API — nothing is sent over the network.
  • Output is downloaded straight from your browser to your computer.

What we store

Only your settings (last width, height, format, quality) in localStorage on your device. Image files are never stored.

How to verify

  1. Open the tool and press F12 to open DevTools.
  2. Switch to the Network tab.
  3. Load and resize an image.
  4. You will see only static assets (the app itself) — no image data leaves your browser.

Hosting

The app is served from Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare only delivers the static files of the app; it never receives or processes your images.
